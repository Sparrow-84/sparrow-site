question: "Can participants keep their job while in the program?"
program: "life-change"
order: 3
---
Yes. LifeChange is designed to work alongside a typical daytime job — program activities happen just twice a week, in the evenings (currently around 4:15 to 6:15pm, though this can change). Sparrow also encourages and helps participants find work, since a steady job is a key part of building lasting financial stability.
