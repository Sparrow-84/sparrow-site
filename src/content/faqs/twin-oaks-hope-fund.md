The Hope Fund is Sparrow's way of stepping in when a Twin Oaks resident faces a sudden crisis — things like a house fire, a major health emergency, or an unexpected loss of heat or essential items. It's meant to help residents stabilize during an emergency, with the goal of preventing a crisis from turning into a loss of housing.

Every situation is different, and we work with each resident individually to see how we can help. If you're facing a hardship or have questions about the Hope Fund, reach out to our team at twinoaks@sparrowinc.org or (541) 753-3334.
