Participants live in Twin Oaks Community (formerly known as Twin Oaks Mobile Home Park, and before that South Corvallis Trailer Park) — 245 SW Twin Oaks Circle, Corvallis, OR 97333 — a community owned and operated by Sparrow.

Housing itself carries no rent for the full twelve months, made possible through generous donations and partnerships with local churches that provide the homes. Every home is different: some are RVs, others are mobile homes, and each one comes fully furnished. A separate monthly program fee applies — see the next question for details.
