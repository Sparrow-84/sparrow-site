Sparrow provides a trained, consistent childcare volunteer on-site during both Monday and Thursday program hours, so moms can fully engage in the program.

On Monday nights during program time, kids attend the [Good News Club](https://www.cefonline.com/ministries/goodnewsclub/), a weekly gathering with pizza for ages 5 to 12, held nearby at Papa's Pizza — the same Sparrow volunteer walks them there and back, and watches any other kids on-site in the meantime. On Thursday nights, kids stay on-site with that same volunteer, with free play time.
