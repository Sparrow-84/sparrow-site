LifeChange offers housing, one-on-one mentorship, financial coaching, and a community that walks with families every step of the way. In return, it asks for real commitment and active participation in mentoring and group sessions.

Two evenings a week, families meet with our team. Monday evenings are one-on-one: mentoring and financial coaching with their own mentor. Thursday evenings are a group session with the other families in the program, centered on peer support and our Built to Last curriculum, with a shared meal every other week. Childcare is provided during both.

We intentionally keep the number of families in the program small so that our staff can give each family real, individual attention — meeting them exactly where they are.

The goal throughout is whole-person growth: physical, emotional, relational, and spiritual, with goals set by and tailored to each family.
