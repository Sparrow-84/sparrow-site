LifeChange is a twelve-month program. We encourage participants to stay for the full twelve months to get the most out of our Built to Last curriculum, one-on-one mentorship, and life skills coaching, but life doesn't always go as planned — we work individually with any participant who needs to leave the program early.

Families who need more time can also request to stay longer than a year. This isn't guaranteed — it's considered based on how well the family has integrated into the program, benefited from it, and attended and participated consistently during the first year.
