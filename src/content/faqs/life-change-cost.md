Housing itself doesn't involve rent.

Our monthly program fee varies by home, generally $300 to $350, plus utilities. Twin Oaks Community covers water and garbage, so those aren't a separate cost. On-site laundry is available using coin-operated machines. Homes aren't equipped with televisions or other electronics, and participants provide their own Wi-Fi if they'd like it.
