question: "How do Sparrow, Twin Oaks Community, and Centurion fit together?"
program: "twin-oaks"
order: 6
---
Sparrow owns Twin Oaks Community and the land it sits on. Centurion Property Management handles the everyday landlord side — lot rent and leases — so residents work with Centurion for those matters. Sparrow's own on-site team — a Caretaker who looks after the community's facilities, and a Resident Services Coordinator — is available to help with things like a maintenance concern, a neighbor situation, or any other question.
