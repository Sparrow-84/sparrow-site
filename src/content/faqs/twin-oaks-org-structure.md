Sparrow is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to walk families out of homelessness and toward lasting stability. We own and operate Twin Oaks Community and the land it sits on, with the goal of making it a safe, stable, and thriving place for residents to call home. Centurion Property Management handles the everyday landlord side — lot rent and leases — so residents work with Centurion for those matters. Sparrow's own on-site team — a Caretaker who looks after the community's facilities, and a Resident Services Coordinator — is available to help with things like a maintenance concern, a neighbor situation, or any other question.
